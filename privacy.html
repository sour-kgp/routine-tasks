Privacy Policy

Routine Tasks · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Routine Tasks (“the app”) is published by Sourabh Karmakar (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What the app stores

  • On your device: your tasks, sub-tasks, completion history, categories, theme and attachments are saved in a folder on your device that you choose.
  • In your Google Drive — only if you choose “Sync with Google”: the same data is stored in a private, hidden folder belonging to the app in your own Google Drive (Google’s “application data folder”). Only Routine Tasks can access it; it does not appear in your Drive file list, and the app cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, the app requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — “See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive”. The app also reads your Google account’s name and email address to show which account is signed in.

  • This data is used only to save and sync your Routine Tasks data between your own devices.
  • It is not sent to us or to any server we operate — the app communicates directly with Google.
  • It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Routine Tasks’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sign-in information

The app keeps a sign-in token on your device so you stay signed in. It never sees or stores your Google password (you sign in on Google’s own page). The token is removed when you choose Sign out, or after 30 days without using the app.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, Settings → Account → Delete cloud data removes everything the app stored in your Google Drive.
  • You can remove the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Local data can be deleted by removing the data folder shown in Settings → Account.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change.
