Routine Tasks

Keep track of the things you do again and again.

Routine Tasks is a to-do app for recurring tasks — watering plants, paying bills, changing filters, weekly reviews. When you tick a task off it isn’t deleted: the app records the completion and schedules the next date from the task’s frequency.

Never lose a routine

Daily to quarterly, or any custom interval in hours, days, weeks, months or years. Full completion history for every task.

Sub-tasks & files

Break a routine into checklists up to three levels deep, and attach any kind of file.

Your look

Light, dark, system default and six styles. Categories with your own icons and colours.

Backups

One-click backup and restore of everything, including attachments.

Sign in with Google (optional)

You can use Routine Tasks entirely on your own computer. If you choose Sync with Google, the app keeps your tasks in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so the same tasks appear on your PC and phone.

  • The app asks for one permission: to see, create and delete its own data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your data goes directly between the app and Google. It is never sent to us.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings → Account) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
